Capabilities

Everything you need. Nothing you don't.

Vesper takes the tedious parts of modded Minecraft — the resolving, the version matching, the folder shuffling — and makes them disappear.

One-click content installation

Search Modrinth and CurseForge from a single bar and install mods, modpacks, resource packs, and shaders straight into the right instance. No manual downloads, no dragging jars into folders, no wondering which site had the newer build.

Modrinth CurseForge Unified search Deduplicated results

Smart mod dependency resolution

Vesper reads each mod's requirements and walks the full tree before anything downloads — pulling required libraries, matching loader and Java versions, and flagging conflicts while they're still fixable instead of after a crash log.

Auto-resolve Conflict detection Version pinning Safe bulk updates

Isolated, fast instances

Every profile gets its own mods, configs, saves, and Java runtime. Nothing bleeds between setups, and a shared artifact cache keeps launches quick no matter how many you keep.

Built-in Discord tab

Server chat and voice sit one tab away from your library, with Rich Presence showing friends which world you're in. Fully opt-in, and off until you turn it on.

Local skin & cape manager

Store, preview, and swap skins and capes from inside the launcher. Your library lives on your disk — no third-party skin host sitting between you and your account.

Privacy-first architecture

No analytics SDK, no ad network, no background phone-home. Instances and tokens stay local, sign-in runs through official Microsoft OAuth, and Vesper only contacts services you explicitly use.
